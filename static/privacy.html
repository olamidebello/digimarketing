Privacy Notice

Effective October 22, 2026. This notice describes data handled by DigiMarketing.

Data collected

We store your email address, a salted password hash, session credentials, campaign briefs, post text, chosen channels, schedule times and delivery results. Your browser stores an essential sign-in cookie. Temporary sign-in attempt records and hosting access logs may include network addresses. Social access tokens are held in a protected server configuration file rather than sent to browsers.

How data is used and shared

Data is used for authentication, campaign management, drafting, scheduling, delivery and troubleshooting. If you request AI drafting, your brief and selected channels are sent to OpenAI. If you approve publication, post content and authentication details are sent to the configured provider. Their privacy terms also apply. We request that AI responses are not stored through the API; provider operational retention may still apply. Do not submit sensitive personal information in campaign briefs.

Retention and deletion

Campaign data remains until you delete it or close your account. Sessions expire after one day. Use account deletion to remove your account, campaigns and delivery records from the active database. Backups and hosting logs may persist under the operator’s configured retention schedule. Deleting local data does not remove content already published to external platforms. Ask the operator to remove any configured social tokens and revoke them with the provider.

Security and choices

The application uses password hashing, session controls and HTTPS when deployed through the supplied configuration. No system is completely secure. You can export campaign text, delete drafts, sign out and delete your account. No optional analytics or advertising cookies are included in this version. Contact the operator through the hosting provider’s published support channel for access or correction requests. Applicable privacy rights depend on your location.
